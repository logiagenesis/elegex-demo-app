# Synthetic Demo Data Catalogue

All data seeded by Elegex is clearly synthetic. It is designed to let a reviewer explore connected job-management workflows without presenting fictional records as real business results.

| Dataset | Typical volume | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Contacts and programmes | 4 each | Client relationship and wider delivery context |
| Jobs | 36 over six months | Booking-to-invoice lifecycle, priority, client, address, and foreman assignment |
| Dispatch visits | 36 | Schedule, travel allowance, field status, and geo-status UI |
| Materials | 72 | Cost and material accountability within job detail |
| Field evidence | 72 | Before-condition, signature, note, sync-status, and audit workflow examples |
| Quotes and quote items | 12 and 24 | Pricing, sent/accepted/needs-pricing status, and conversion reporting |
| Invoice links | 23 | External-accounting reference rather than duplicated invoice storage |
| Monthly snapshots | 6 | Recharts operational and commercial trend views |
| Release evidence | 3 environments, 5 staging checks | Staging-readiness control surface |
| Documents | 3 managed text artifacts | Job card, quote, and safety checklist links in the document module |
