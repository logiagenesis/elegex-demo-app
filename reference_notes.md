# Elegex Reference Notes

The supplied reference presents a light-first field-service and job-management product. Its office experience uses a narrow left navigation rail, a calm cool-gray canvas, white cards with restrained borders, and a cobalt blue action color. The visual hierarchy pairs condensed, high-contrast job or metric labels with compact uppercase supporting labels and modest status chips.

The reference dashboard offers five operational measures: completed, in progress, upcoming, quotes open, and invoiced. It combines a conversion indicator with an exception-focused “Needs attention” panel. The sidebar groups operational sections such as Dashboard, Jobs, Calendar, Invoicing, Quotes, Clients, Foremen, Catalogue, and Settings, with a persistent New Job action. The implementation will adapt this concise operational vocabulary into an original multi-tenant business-management interface focused on projects, cases, contacts, tasks, documents, reports, and administration.

The field-facing mock emphasizes legibility, scheduled work, clear job status, and obvious next actions. The rebuilt web product will preserve the reference’s clarity, light-first palette, restrained use of color, and direct operational affordances while providing the broader functionality requested for the database-backed demo.
