ACCEPTANCE RETRACTED

The final acceptance report was reissued after the audit established that its foreman claim lacked functional evidence.

The prior decision treated a rendered route as acceptance evidence. That was not sufficient.
What happened

Why it failed

Consent, location capture, evidence, signature, completion-with-gaps, and queue drain were not functionally verified.
Corrective action
