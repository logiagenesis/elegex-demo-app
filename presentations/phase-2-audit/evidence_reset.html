Two documents made mutually exclusive claims about the same foreman workflow.

Prior acceptance report
“Field-service lifecycle and foreman workflow” was marked accepted.
It cited consent, check-in/out, materials, evidence, completion, and sync as persistence-backed flows.
Reference UX analysis
