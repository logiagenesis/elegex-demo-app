QUEUE
SAFETY
CONTROLS

Offline safety is not a single retry button. It is a coordinated set of persistence, ordering, binary, and failure-management controls.
Every queue item must protect the foreman's completed work from network loss, replay, and silent failure.
Risk scenarioRequired control
Interrupted networkExponential backoff with jitter, capped retries, and an actionable failure state.
Replay after reconnectionClient-generated UUID idempotency keys; server deduplication by organization and key.
Out-of-order job updatesExplicit dependencies: check-in before materials; materials and evidence before completion.
Large photos and signaturesPersist blobs in IndexedDB—not base64 Data URLs—before uploading to storage.
Permanent failureSurface a job-language remediation item; never silently discard a mutation.
Required assurance: replaying a completed queue must produce zero duplicate rows and preserve the original causal order.
